This site is a browsable catalog ir-datasets, a Python library that provides easy access to Information Retrieval (IR) datasets. It handles downloading, extracting, and reading source files, exposing document, query, and relevance judgment collections through a common interface, linked to one another in a graph.

Datasets are organized into four types:

Resources are raw sources, like downloadable files. Example: irds:cranfield.tar.gz

Tables parse Resources into records with the same structure. Example: irds:msmarco-passage

Benchmarks are combinations of Tables to evaluate a specific task. Example: irds:antique-test

Suites are collections of Benchmarks that are evaluated together. Example: irds:bright
